ChargeRW

Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

ChargeRW is an app and website for finding electric-vehicle chargers in Kigali and following your charge. It is run by Sela Energy Ltd, Kigali, Rwanda (“we”). This policy explains what we collect, why, who sees it, and how to delete it. Questions: denysmahoro@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address (we email you a code to sign in), your name, and if you add them, your Mobile Money number, your vehicle model and its battery size.
  • Your charges: for each charge, the station and charger, when it started and ended, the battery level you entered, the station’s timer and whether it finished or was cancelled.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, a notification token for your device, so we can tell you when your charge is nearly done and when it is finished.
  • Sign-in codes: a scrambled copy (a hash) of each code we email you, and how many codes your address asked for. We never store the code itself.

Your location stays on your device. If you allow it, the app uses your location to show the nearest chargers and how far away they are. We don’t store it or send it to our servers. On the website, the map images come from OpenStreetMap, whose servers see which part of the map you look at.

We don’t collect your contacts, photos or browsing history. We don’t show ads and we don’t sell your data.

How we use it

  • to sign you in and keep you signed in;
  • to let the station start your charge, and to show you its progress;
  • to send you notifications about your charge;
  • to keep the station’s records of its charges.

Who sees it

The manager of the station where you charge sees your name, the battery level you entered and your Mobile Money number (if you added it) for your charges at their station, so they can start your charge and contact you. They may also get a text message with your name when you say you are plugged in. Other drivers see nothing about you.

These companies process data for us, only to run ChargeRW:

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, the database and our server functions. The database is in Google’s data centre in South Africa, and our server functions run in Belgium.
  • Brevo: sends the sign-in code emails, so it receives your email address.
  • Pindo: sends text messages to station managers, which can include your name.
  • Expo: delivers notifications to your phone, using your notification token.
  • GitHub: hosts this website.

How long we keep it

  • Your account is kept until you delete it.
  • Records of your charges are kept for the station’s accounts. When you delete your account, your name and Mobile Money number are removed from them.
  • A sign-in code works for 5 minutes and only once. Its record is removed when you sign in with it, or replaced when you ask for a new one. Daily totals of codes sent are kept without any address.

Deleting your account

Sign in to the app or the website, open your account and tap Delete my account. If you can’t sign in, email us from the address you use with ChargeRW. See how to delete your account.

Your choices

You can turn off location and notifications in your phone’s settings at any time; the app still works without them. You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it or to delete it by emailing us. We reply within 30 days.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our database rules let each driver read only their own profile and charges, and each station manager only their own station’s charges.

Children

ChargeRW is for drivers and is not meant for children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update this page and the date at the top.
