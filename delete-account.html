Delete your account

You can delete your ChargeRW account yourself, in the app or on this website.

In the app or on the website

  1. Open ChargeRW and sign in with your email.
  2. Tap the round account button at the top right.
  3. Tap Delete my account, then Yes, delete my account.
Sign in on the website

What is deleted

  • your name, email address, Mobile Money number, vehicle and battery size;
  • your notification token and your places on station waiting lists;
  • your sign-in and any sign-in code.

What is kept

Records of your past charges stay with the stations for their accounts, without your name or Mobile Money number. You can’t delete your account while a charge is still running.

Can’t sign in?

Email denysmahoro@gmail.com from the address you use with ChargeRW, with the subject “Delete my ChargeRW account”. We delete your account within 30 days and reply when it is done.
